Features

Any SIP carrier

UDP, TCP and TLS, Digest registration, several trunks with priorities, works behind NAT.

Six scenarios

Plain message, with confirmation, with a recorded reply, looped, looped with confirmation, siren.

Key-press confirmation

"0" confirms, "1" repeats the message. Extra answer options on keys 2 and 3.

Start by phone

Inbound call, access code, choose the alert — start it or record a new message.

Structure and contacts

Department tree, alert locations, contacts, schedules, holiday calendar, CSV import.

Roles and audit

Operators, roles and a permission matrix. Every start and stop is logged.

Reliable service

The core runs as a Windows service with automatic restart, live status and diagnostics.

Your data stays with you

Embedded PostgreSQL on your server. No cloud services, no contact lists leave your network.

FAQ

Does it need the internet or a cloud service?

No. AlertLine runs on your server. Only the calls leave your network, through the SIP carrier you choose.

Which carriers does it work with?

Any carrier or PBX that supports SIP, with registration (login and password) or IP authentication, over UDP, TCP or TLS.

How many calls run at once?

As many as your licence allows and your carrier provides channels for. Standard — up to 30 lines, Business — unlimited.

Can I record my own message?

Yes: upload WAV or MP3 in the operator console, or record it by calling the system with an access code.

What happens when the licence expires?

Alerting does not stop: the system keeps working in demo mode on two lines while you renew the key.

What must AlertLine not be used for?

Unsolicited marketing calls, fraud and any calls the law forbids. This is part of the licence agreement.

How do I pay?

Organisations pay by invoice and contract via bank transfer. The 30-day trial key is free after a short request.
